Rules

Logging requirements, provisional license limits, and hardship information.

Review before every practice session.

  • ⏱️
    2-hour daily cap. Driving is recorded for no more than 2 hours per day. Any time beyond that is not counted.
  • 🌙
    10 night hours. 10 hours of the required total must be completed at night.
  • 🧑‍✈️
    Qualified supervisor. Must be 21 or older, hold a valid driver's license, and sit in the front passenger seat during every session.
  • ✍️
    Parent sign-off. Each session is reviewed and approved by a parent before it counts.

These apply once the Provisional License is issued.

🌙
Curfew: no driving midnight to 5:00 AM.Violations can have serious consequences.
👥
One passenger max.No more than ONE passenger under 21 who is NOT a family member.
📵
Zero tolerance for wireless devices.No phone use of any kind, including hands-free and voice features. Put the phone away before the car moves.

Must-dos

  • 🔒
    Wear a seat belt, and make sure every passenger does too.
  • 🪪
    Carry the Provisional License whenever you drive.
  • 🚦
    Obey posted speed limits and traffic signals.
  • ↔️
    Keep a safe following distance and signal every turn and lane change.

Do's & don'ts

  • 👍
    Do plan routes ahead and set GPS before moving.
  • 🅿️
    Do pull over somewhere safe to adjust anything or reply to a message.
  • 🚫
    Don't drive drowsy, upset, or impaired by alcohol, drugs, or medication.
  • 🎧
    Don't let music, food, or passengers pull your attention from the road.

Reference only. Confirm current eligibility and forms directly with the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS).

🎓 Impact Texas Teen Drivers (ITTD)Free DPS video course · required
  • Required before the road test for teens who took driver education, including parent-taught.
  • Take it after finishing the behind-the-wheel hours.
  • The completion certificate must be dated within 90 days of the road test.
  • It's an 8-part video course. Print the certificate and bring it to DPS.

📝 TDLR form DES150N: 30-hour logOfficial PDF
  • The official 30-hour behind-the-wheel log. Bring it to DPS with the DE-964 certificate.
  • At least 10 of the 30 hours must be at night.
  • Only 2 hours per day count toward the 30 hours.
  • Owl Driver's Export for Texas DPS (Log tab) fills in the same columns.

  • Parents with a "B" restriction on their license can't be the instructor.

Laws to know

🚦 Graduated Driver License rules (under 18)Texas DPS
  • Learner license: must be held at least 6 months, always with a licensed adult.
  • Provisional: no more than one passenger under 21 who isn't family.
  • Curfew: no driving midnight to 5:00 AM, except for work, school, or emergencies.
  • Phones: no wireless device use while driving, including hands-free.
  • A suspension during the learner phase adds that time to the 6 months.
Read the DPS GDL rules ↗
🚫 Zero tolerance: alcohol under 21Texas law · TABC
  • Under 21, driving with any detectable amount of alcohol is illegal.
  • 1st offense: up to $500 fine, alcohol awareness class, 20–40 hours of community service, 60-day license suspension.
  • 2nd offense: up to $500 fine, 40–60 hours of community service, 120-day suspension.
  • 3rd offense: 180-day suspension. Age 17+ faces $500–$2,000 and/or up to 180 days in jail.
