Logging rules

Review before every practice session.

  • ⏱️
    2-hour daily cap. Driving is recorded for no more than 2 hours per day. Any time beyond that is not counted.
  • 🌙
    10 night hours. 10 hours of the required total must be completed at night.
  • 🧑‍✈️
    Qualified supervisor. Must be 21 or older, hold a valid driver's license, and sit in the front passenger seat during every session.
  • ✍️
    Parent sign-off. Each session is reviewed and approved by a parent before it counts.

Provisional license

These apply once the Provisional License is issued.

🌙
Curfew: no driving midnight to 5:00 AM.Violations can have serious consequences.
👥
One passenger max.No more than ONE passenger under 21 who is NOT a family member.
📵
Zero tolerance for wireless devices.No phone use of any kind, including hands-free and voice features. Put the phone away before the car moves.

Must-dos

  • 🔒
    Wear a seat belt, and make sure every passenger does too.
  • 🪪
    Carry the Provisional License whenever you drive.
  • 🚦
    Obey posted speed limits and traffic signals.
  • ↔️
    Keep a safe following distance and signal every turn and lane change.

Do's & don'ts

  • 👍
    Do plan routes ahead and set GPS before moving.
  • 🅿️
    Do pull over somewhere safe to adjust anything or reply to a message.
  • 🚫
    Don't drive drowsy, upset, or impaired by alcohol, drugs, or medication.
  • 🎧
    Don't let music, food, or passengers pull your attention from the road.
